Key features / innovation
Find beyond lineageExact edges plus receiver-scoped semantic sketches and FHIR support fingerprints.
Require causal proofLocal counterfactual replay asks whether the patient or action actually changes.
Rebuild from sourceConfirmed descendants are reconstructed from canonical FHIR or held for review.

Privacy design: raw content stays with its owning role. Capsule fields are purpose- and recipient-scoped; recovery decisions are auditable and independently verifiable.

Social / environmental impact

Social: aims to reduce the persistence of wrong-patient and unauthorised clinical-agent memory while retaining useful, unrelated context and respecting role boundaries.
Environmental: targeted repair may avoid repeated full-memory resets and recomputation; energy savings have not yet been measured.

Future scope / expected outcome

Clinician-supervised usability study · governed FHIR sandbox integration · stronger privacy-preserving fingerprints · calibrated thresholds · larger external synthetic cohorts.
